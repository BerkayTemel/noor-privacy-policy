Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Noor: Prayer and Qibla is developed by Byinves. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our services:

  • Location data: Used solely to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for your location. Location data is processed on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Device information: Basic device information for app functionality and crash reporting.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal identification information
  • We do not collect financial information
  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not track your browsing history
  • We do not require account registration

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate accurate prayer times based on your location
  • To determine the Qibla direction
  • To send prayer time notifications (only if you enable this feature)
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

4. Data Storage

All data (prayer logs, settings, dhikr counts) is stored locally on your device only. We do not store any of your personal data on external servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Our app may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Maps: For nearby mosque features (subject to Google's Privacy Policy)
  • Anthropic Claude AI: For the AI assistant feature (queries are processed by Anthropic's API)
  • Google Fonts: For typography

6. Location Permission

The app requests location permission to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. You can deny this permission, but prayer time calculations will not be available. Location data is only used on-device and is never shared with third parties.

7. Notifications

If you enable prayer time notifications, the app will send local notifications based on calculated prayer times. These notifications are generated locally on your device.

8. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages and contains no harmful content.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
